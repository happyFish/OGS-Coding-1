How to Play

  • Use Left/Right arrows to move
  • Use Up arrow or Space to jump
  • Use Down arrow or S to duck
  • Collect coins and power-ups
  • Jump over or duck under obstacles
  • Buy shoes (slower) or lives (extra chance) in the shop
  • Special power-ups let you jump higher or duck longer
